Wu wei means effortless action.

It's an old Taoist idea: don't force the day, let it unfold. That's the whole plan on board. We handle the boat, the route and the timing. You decide whether the next hour is a swim, a nap on the bow or another round of drinks while the sun drops behind the Twelve Apostles.

Skippered by Bernado, who knows this coast and its moods, and who'll happily tell you the story behind the name.

Good to know

What to bring
Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen and a warm layer for the trip home. The Atlantic is cold even in summer, so the brave get the bragging rights.
Weather
The skipper calls it the morning of the trip. If the wind or swell is up, we move your date at no cost. Safety first, every time.
Children
Welcome. Life jackets in kids' sizes are on board, and the swim platform makes getting in and out easy.
Food and drinks
Bring your own, or ask us to arrange platters and a cooler box. There's a fridge on board. Glass is fine, just mind the deck.
Seasickness
We stay close to the coast in calm water, so most people are fine. If you're prone to it, take something an hour before.
Safety
Licensed skipper, life jackets for everyone and full safety gear on board. (Bernado: survey and permit details go here.)
