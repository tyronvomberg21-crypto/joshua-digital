The House

True Cape hospitality, behind the gable

Step through the front doors of Malherbe and the whole valley opens up in front of you. Behind this monumental 1859 Cape Dutch house, Joop and Micky Meijer have made a home for travellers — a secret garden of contemporary rooms, restored with an eye for every detail.

A glass of local Montagu Muscadel to welcome you in, a pool tucked among the greenery, and breakfast the next morning under the vines — homemade bread, fruit, and eggs cooked exactly to order (Joop, guests will tell you, is a scrambled-egg expert).

The Garden

The garden guests call a small paradise

Hidden behind the house is a secluded garden with a pool, shaded seating and the Langeberg mountains rising beyond — the quiet heart of Malherbe. It's where the day winds down and where breakfast is laid out beneath the vines each morning.

Peaceful, green, and entirely private — the kind of place people book to come back to.

Montagu

A village of heritage, springs and wine

Montagu sits where the Breede River Valley meets Route 62 — a walkable village of 25 heritage houses, ringed by mountains and wineries, with its famous hot springs a few minutes away.

Hot SpringsThe celebrated Montagu mineral hot springs, a short drive from the door — a soak with a mountain view.
Route 62 WineOver 30 wineries within 25 km, plus muscadel and port houses along the world's longest wine route.
Mountains & HistoryHiking trails into the Langeberg, and 25 historical houses to wander in the village itself.
