The corner café

Coffee at dawn.
Crêpes all day.

The doors open at 6:40 for the dawn-patrol surfers. By mid-morning it's crêpes, smoothies and the best seat in Muizenberg: the beach huts across the road and the whole bay behind them.

01

Proper coffee, first thing

Flat whites that regulars rave about, a hot chocolate people drive out for, plus cold-pressed juices and health shakes when the sun's up.

02

The famous crêpes

Sweet or salty, thin and golden. The "What the Fudge" with homemade fudge, banana and toasted almonds is the one everyone orders first.

03

The view, and the vibe

Colourful, relaxed, a little bit funky. Surfers, dogs, laptops and families all fit. Sit outside and watch the line-up while you eat.
