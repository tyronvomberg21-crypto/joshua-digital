The House

Owner-run warmth in the Winelands

The idea behind African Tulip is a simple one: make yourself at home, and let the Witzenberg Valley do the rest. Elise and Frank have hosted here since 2016 — a Dutch couple who fell for Tulbagh and now run one of its most-loved guest houses, hands-on, every day.

Four rooms, each with its own character. A garden and a generous pool to come home to. And a buffet breakfast that turns up again and again in the reviews as the best part of the stay.

Tulbagh

Walk to Church Street, stay for the valley

African Tulip sits at 8 Meiring Street — a few minutes on foot from Tulbagh's historic Church Street, the longest row of provincial heritage sites in the country, lined with restaurants, wine tasting and galleries.

Ninety minutes from Cape Town, wrapped by the Witzenberg mountains on three sides: wine farms, hiking, cycling and clear country nights.

Church StreetA short walk to the country's longest street of heritage buildings — restaurants, cafés and galleries.
Wine & FarmsThe Tulbagh wine route on the doorstep — cellars, tastings and long valley lunches.
MountainsHiking and cycling in the Witzenberg range, and some of the darkest, starriest skies in the Cape.
