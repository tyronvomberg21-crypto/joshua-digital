Shopify or custom

Do I need Shopify?

No. Shops can run on Shopify or fully custom, and I'll tell you which fits on the first chat — it depends on how many products you have and how you want to run the shop day to day.

Shopify earns its place when you have a long product list with sizes and options, stock you need to keep track of, or you want to run the shop from the Shopify app on your phone. I design the site around it so it looks like yours, not a theme, and Shopify bills you its own plan directly. A shorter range — a dozen cakes, a seasonal flower list, a small menu — is often better as a fully custom shop: hand-coded in plain HTML, nothing to break, no monthly platform fee, with card payments or orders straight to your WhatsApp.

The shop I built for Café Chiffon, a Green Point dessert café, was built on Shopify, with Yoco for card payments and a collection-date picker built into the product page — no extra app, no extra monthly fee. You can see the design on the work page.

  • Shopify fits when: lots of products, sizes and options, stock to track, or you want the Shopify app on your phone
  • Custom fits when: a shorter range, a menu or seasonal list, and you'd rather have no platform subscription at all
  • Either way: designed from scratch, card payments to your own bank, your own editor, domain in your name
Payments

Where does the money go?

Straight to your bank. Card payments run through your own payment provider — Yoco or PayFast — opened in your name, so the money never passes through me and I never touch the banking side.

The provider pays out to your own bank account and takes its own small fee per transaction, which you see in your own dashboard, not mine. I set it up, run a test order, and we take one real order before launch so you've seen the money land. On a custom shop without card payments, the order arrives on your WhatsApp and you take payment the way you do now.

  • Your Yoco or PayFast account, in your name, with your login
  • Payouts to your own bank account — I'm not in the middle of your money
  • Set up and tested before launch: test mode first, then one real order
  • If you're on Shopify, Shopify bills its plan to you directly as well
Your editor

Can I add products myself?

Yes. On Shopify you add products, change prices and mark things sold out from the Shopify app on your phone — it's your store and your login. On a custom shop, every site comes with a simple editor so you change your own words, prices and photos.

The editor is an email invite, no account to set up: a plain form with just your words and photos, so the layout stays put. Change a price, tap Save, live in about a minute — see it here. And if you'd rather just WhatsApp me the change, the care plan covers your updates — new products, prices, photos, specials and seasonal ranges — done within the week, up to about an hour of work a month. Bigger jobs, like a new page, are quoted upfront.

  • Shopify: products, prices, stock and sold-out from the app on your phone
  • Custom: your own editor for words, prices and photos, no account to set up
  • Care plan: new products, specials and seasonal updates done within the week
  • Without the care plan: changes are R350 an hour, and the site stays yours either way

Online shop questions

Do I own the shop, or do you?
You do. The domain is registered in your name, your Shopify, Yoco or PayFast accounts are yours with your own login, and at launch I email you a copy of the whole site. The care plan runs month to month — cancel any time with a month's notice and the shop stays yours and keeps working.
I already take orders on WhatsApp and Instagram — why do I need an online shop?
Keep them — the shop works with them. It catches the people who Google you before buying, shows your full range with real prices and sizes while you're busy, and takes the order and the payment without you typing a word. Every order it handles is one you didn't have to stop and answer. Ask me anything on WhatsApp.
What does it cost to keep an online shop running?
Very little from my side. There's no hosting bill, your domain is about R99 a year in your own name (covered while you're on the care plan), and the care plan is R450 a month from launch, first month free — your updates, a monthly check that everything works, domain renewal and WhatsApp support, direct to me. If the shop runs on Shopify, Shopify bills you its own monthly plan directly, and your payment provider takes a small fee per card payment — both in your name, neither through me.
What if I get too busy or sell out of something?
On Shopify you mark a product sold out, set it to draft, or raise your notice days from the app on your phone — one tap each. On a custom shop you change prices and words in your editor, and for anything bigger you WhatsApp me and it's done within the week on the care plan.
What do you need from me to start?
Your product list with prices and sizes, your photos, a few lines about the business, and your logo if you have one — plus half of the build price to start. If you don't have words or a logo yet, written copy is R400 and a text logo is R500 as optional add-ons. From the day I have your content, the shop is live in about 7 days.
