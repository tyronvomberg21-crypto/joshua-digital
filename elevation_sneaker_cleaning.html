Good To Know

Questions, answered

Do you really pick up and drop off?
Yep — free pickup & delivery anywhere in our zones (Lansdowne, Kenwyn, Milnerton & nearby). Outside those? Message us, we'll sort something out.
How long does it take?
Most cleans are back with you within about 48 hours. Restorations can take a little longer — we'll let you know upfront.
Can you clean suede / mesh / knit?
Absolutely. We match the method and products to your sneaker's material, so delicate uppers are safe with us.
Will it damage my sneakers?
Never. Everything is done by hand with sneaker-safe products — no harsh machines, no risk.
