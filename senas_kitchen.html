a little story

French-inspired artistry, a proudly SA twist 🇿🇦

Sena's Kitchen brings the world of Cédric Grolet — the famed Parisian pastry chef — to South Africa, with sculptural fruit entremets, silky mousses and surprise centres in every bite.

Every dessert is handcrafted in small batches, finished by hand, and made to look as breathtaking as it tastes.
