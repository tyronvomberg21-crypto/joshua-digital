Our story

Mac's Spice started as a secret family recipe, shaken over braais and Sunday roasts for friends and family since the 1960s.

In the mid-nineties the family bottled it, stuck a fun, proudly South African label on it and gave it away at Christmas and birthdays. People kept asking for more.

In 2019 John McClelland, Cape Town boytjie and founder, took it to the local market. It went from one 100 ml pot of The Original to a range of thirteen products, blended and packed in a certified Cape Town facility.

Good to know

How long does the spice last?

24 months from blending. Keep the lid closed and the shaker out of the sun and away from the steam of the pot.

Is it Halal and Kosher?

Yes. Every product in the range is Halal and Kosher certified, and the facility is BRCGS accredited.

What about allergens?

Several blends contain mustard seed, and the marinades contain sulphites. Everything is packed in a facility that also handles wheat, soya, milk, egg, celery, sesame and mustard. Tap any product for its full list.
