The House

Shirley's place, and she'll make it yours

Set high in Protea Heights, Shirley's Place has been a home-from-home for travellers for years — the kind of spot where the host knows your name, breakfast is worth waking for, and a woman travelling alone tells you she felt completely safe.

Six rooms, en-suite and traveller options, all with tea & coffee, TV and heaters. An indoor pool for any weather, a braai for the evenings, secure parking behind the gate, and even an airport shuttle if you need one.
