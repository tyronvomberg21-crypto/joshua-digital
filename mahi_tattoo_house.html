The Studio

Quiet craft

Mahi Tattoo House is a boutique studio in Cape Town founded by Tyler Siebert, dedicated to the art of the line. No flash walls, no rush — just considered, custom fine-line work in a calm, private space.

We specialise in delicate botanical, illustrative and single-needle tattoos: pieces that look as good in ten years as they do on day one. Every design is drawn for you, around your idea, your body and your story.

Open weekends, by appointment only. Walk-ins aren't really our thing — your piece deserves more thought than that.

— Tyler, Mahi Tattoo House
