Who We Are

Born frisky in 2021

Frisky Goat started during lockdown with one obsession: roasting coffee good enough to earn the name G.O.A.T — Greatest Of All Time. It's also a wink to the legend of Khaldi, the Ethiopian goat-herder whose goats first discovered the buzz of the coffee cherry.

Every batch is roasted by hand in Thornton on our 6kg Genio roaster — affectionately named Frisky #1 — using only specialty-grade, ethically sourced 100% arabica beans. No shortcuts, no bitterness, no nonsense.

Pop into our café for a fresh cup and a toastie, grab a bag of beans, or let us roast for your business. However you take it, we roast it to be the best in the room.
