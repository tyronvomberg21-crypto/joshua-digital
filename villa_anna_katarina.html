The House

A home hung with paintings, kept by the painter

Villa Anna Katarina is Lizette's house — an artist's home on Fontein Street where her own canvases hang in every room, French wingbacks sit beside exposed brick, and each suite has been composed rather than decorated.

Guests write about the peace, the views, and Lizette going above and beyond — some even get a look inside her studio. It's a five-minute stroll down to the square's restaurants and galleries, and a slow climb back up to a balcony, a glass of Swartland wine, and the sunset.

The Art

Sleep inside a gallery

The paintings on the walls aren't prints — they're Lizette's. Horses in blue, figures in charcoal, canvases that make each room unmistakably itself. Guests who ask nicely have been known to get a tour of the studio.

It's the kind of character no chain, and no booking-site listing, can reproduce — you have to stay here to see it.

The Village

Riebeek-Kasteel, 400 metres below

The Swartland's prettiest village — a square ringed with restaurants, galleries and wine bars, olive farms and cellars in every direction, and mountains behind it all.

The SquareA five-minute stroll to the village square — restaurants, galleries, boutiques and the famous Sunday slow lunches.
Wine & OlivesSwartland wine estates and olive farms on the doorstep — tastings, harvest lunches and cellar doors.
The ValleyMountain-bike and 4x4 trails, game drives at Bartholomeus Klip, and that sunset — every single evening.
