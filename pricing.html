Common questions

Why monthly instead of one big payment?
It keeps your upfront cost low and means your site is always hosted, updated and looked after — you're never left with a site that breaks and no one to fix it. If it brings you one extra customer a month, it's already paid for itself.
Do I own my website?
Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours. No lock-in contracts — cancel whenever you like.
How long does it take?
Most one-page sites go live within a few days of getting your details and photos. Bigger sites take about a week.
What if I need changes later?
That's what the monthly covers — just WhatsApp me your changes (new prices, photos, hours) and I'll handle them.
