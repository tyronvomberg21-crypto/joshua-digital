Common questions

Do I really need a website — lots of businesses just use Instagram?
Instagram and Facebook are great, but you're renting someone else's platform — they can change the algorithm, go down, or suspend your account, and you'd lose everything. A website is yours. It also shows up on Google when someone searches "barber near me" or "birthday cake Cape Town", which Instagram can't do. For most local businesses, that Google presence alone is worth it. Still unsure? Ask me on WhatsApp.
Why monthly instead of one big payment?
It keeps your upfront cost low and means your site is always hosted, updated and looked after — you're never left with a site that breaks and no one to fix it. If it brings you one extra customer a month, it's already paid for itself.
Is R299 a month actually worth it?
One new customer who found you on Google covers the whole month. If your site brings you even one extra booking a month, it's already paid for itself — and it usually brings more.
Do I own my website?
Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours. No lock-in contracts — cancel whenever you like.
How long does it take?
Most one-page sites go live within a few days of getting your details and photos. Bigger sites take about a week.
Can you do my photos and videos too?
Yes — on the Pro plan (or as an add-on) I arrange a professional photo & video shoot through a trusted Cape Town photography partner. I brief them on your brand and coordinate the whole thing, so your site looks incredible with real, original content.
What if I need changes later?
That's what the monthly covers — just WhatsApp me your changes (new prices, photos, hours) and I'll handle them.
