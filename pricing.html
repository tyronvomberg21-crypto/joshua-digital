Common questions

I already take orders on WhatsApp and Instagram — why do I need a website?
Keep them! The website works with them. It catches the people who Google you before buying, shows your full menu or products with real prices while you're busy, and takes the order or booking without you typing a word. Every order it handles is one you didn't have to stop and answer. Ask me anything on WhatsApp.
Will it work with the tools I already use?
Yes. If you take bookings on Fresha, Setmore, NightsBridge or similar, your "Book" button plugs straight into it — nothing breaks, no double bookings. Shops can run on Shopify or fully custom. And if you use none of that, orders and enquiries come straight to your WhatsApp.
Do I own my website?
Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours. No lock-in contracts — the care plan is optional and you can cancel whenever you like.
How long does it take?
From go-ahead, most sites are live in about a week to ten days once I have your photos and details. You see the design early and shape it with me — nothing goes live until you love it.
What does the R350/month care plan cover?
Hosting, domain management, and your updates — new products, prices, photos, specials, changed hours. You WhatsApp me the change, I make it, usually same week. If you'd rather manage it yourself, that's fine too — the site is yours either way.
What does it cost to keep running?
Very little. Hosting is free on the setup I use, and your domain costs about R99 a year, paid in your own name so it's always yours. The optional R350/month care plan is the only ongoing cost — and it's there so you never have to think about any of this.
