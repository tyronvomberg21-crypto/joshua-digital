The House

Small, personal, and genuinely looked after

Mabet & Gabriella isn't a hotel — it's two beautifully kept guest rooms on 2B Le Roux Street, one of Robertson's prettiest oak-lined roads. A chalkboard with your name on it at the door, a chocolate on the turned-down towels, and hosts who'll point you to the right cellar and book your table.

Air-conditioned, en-suite, DStv, fast Wi-Fi, and safe private parking behind the gate. Everything you need for a wine-country weekend, nothing you don't.

Breakfast

The breakfast guests write home about

Ask the night before and you'll wake to a spread worth getting up for: a full cooked breakfast with bacon, boerewors and grilled tomato, warm croissants and pain au chocolat, fruit, granola and yoghurt, olives, and proper cafetière coffee.

One guest put it best — the hostess "cooks an awesome breakfast, beyond scrumptious." It's an optional extra, and one nobody regrets.

The Valley

The heart of the Robertson wine route

You're walking distance from the village and a short drive from some of the Cape's most generous cellars — the "valley of wine and roses" on Route 62.

Wine & CellarsSpringfield, Graham Beck, De Wetshof and dozens more along the Breede River — tastings, long lunches and MCC by the case.
The VillageA 15-minute stroll to the Robertson Art Gallery, restaurants and coffee shops; the golf club is minutes away.
Route 62The world's longest wine route runs through the door — Montagu, McGregor and the Klein Karoo all within an easy drive.
