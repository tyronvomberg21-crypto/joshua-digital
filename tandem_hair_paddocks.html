The Salon

Where blonde belongs

Tucked inside Paddocks Centre in Milnerton, Tandem Hair is an owner-run salon led by Joanne Van Rensburg — a stylist with a serious passion for colour. We specialise in the tricky, beautiful stuff: balayage, blonde, highlights and colour corrections done right.

Every appointment starts with a proper consultation, because great colour is personal. We take the time to understand your hair, your tone and your lifestyle — then create a look that grows out gracefully and turns heads.

Come sit in our chair. You're in expert hands.

— Joanne & the Tandem team

Good To Know

Questions, answered

Where are you?
Inside Paddocks Centre, Racecourse Road, Milnerton, Cape Town — easy parking right outside.
Do I need a consultation first?
For colour, we always start with a quick consultation so we can plan the right approach and give you an accurate quote — especially for balayage and corrections.
How do I book?
Easiest is a quick WhatsApp or book online via Fresha. For colour corrections, message us a photo of your hair and we'll advise.
What are your hours?
Mon–Fri 9am–6pm, Sat 9am–4pm, Sun 10am–2pm. Booking ahead is recommended, especially for colour.
