The Studio

Your me-time, perfected

Welcome to Polish Me — a boutique nail studio tucked away in Wynberg, where it's just you, good vibes, and a fresh set going on. No rushed conveyor-belt salon energy here.

I specialise in long-lasting gel, gel & acrylic extensions and custom nail art — from clean and classy to full glam. Every set is shaped, prepped and finished properly, so your nails stay flawless for weeks.

Booked through a quick WhatsApp, done in a relaxed space, and posted to the gram before you've even left. Come treat yourself.
