Connections

Which booking tools can it connect to?

The ones you already use. If you take bookings on Fresha, Setmore, NightsBridge or a similar tool, your "Book" button plugs straight into it: your diary, your availability and your confirmations stay exactly where they are, nothing breaks, and there are no double bookings because the tool carries on holding the calendar. The website's job is to get the customer to that button at 11pm, when they're deciding.

If you don't use a booking tool at all, bookings come straight to your WhatsApp and you confirm them the way you do now.

  • Salons, barbers and beauty: Fresha, Setmore or similar. The Book button opens your live diary.
  • Guesthouses and B&Bs: NightsBridge or similar. Direct stays on your own site, alongside your Booking.com listing.
  • Restaurants and cafés: table reservations to the tool you use, or straight to WhatsApp.
  • No tool yet? Bookings land in your WhatsApp. If you start using one later, I point the Book button at it on the care plan.
Payments

Can customers pay online?

Yes, in one of two ways. If your Book button hands the customer to your existing tool, any deposit is taken by that tool and lands in the account you already have with it; the website doesn't touch the money. If card payments run on the site, they go straight to your bank through a payment provider in your own name: Yoco, PayFast or Shopify.

Either way the money never passes through me. Tell me which tool you use and I'll check what it can do before I promise anything, and I'll tell you on the first chat which of the two sites fits how you take bookings today.

  • Through your booking tool: deposits handled by the tool, if it offers them.
  • On the site: card payments straight to your bank via Yoco, PayFast or Shopify.
  • Not sure your tool does deposits? Send me its name on WhatsApp and I'll check it first.

Booking website questions

I already take bookings on Fresha and Instagram — do I still need a website?
Keep them. The website works with them: it catches the people who Google you before they book, shows your services, prices and hours while you're busy with a client, and sends them to your Fresha diary (or your WhatsApp) without you typing a word. Every booking it handles is one DM you didn't have to stop and answer.
Will it cause double bookings?
No. Your Book button plugs straight into the tool you already use, so the tool carries on holding your diary and your availability; the website doesn't keep a second calendar. Nothing breaks, no double bookings. If bookings come to your WhatsApp instead, you confirm each one the way you do now.
What if I don't use a booking tool at all?
Then bookings come straight to your WhatsApp. And if you start using Fresha, Setmore or NightsBridge later, I point the Book button at it on the care plan.
Do I own the site, and can I change my prices and hours myself?
Yes to both. Your domain is registered in your name, you get a copy of the whole site emailed to you at launch, and every site includes a simple editor: change a price or your hours from your phone, tap Save, live in about a minute. The care plan runs month to month, and cancelling it doesn't switch anything off. See the editor.
What does a booking website cost to keep running?
Hosting is free on the setup I use, and your domain costs about R99 a year in your own name (covered while you're on the care plan). The R450 a month care plan is the only ongoing cost: included from launch, first month free, cancel any time with a month's notice. Without it, changes are R350 an hour.
