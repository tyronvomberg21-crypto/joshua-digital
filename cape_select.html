The House

Space to gather, room to breathe

Cape Select sits on Aandblom Road in the Durbanville Wine Valley — a self-catering guest house built for people who'd rather stay together than scatter across hotel rooms. Five bedrooms, a lounge that opens to a view balcony, and a fully equipped country kitchen at the heart of it all.

Outside: a pool, sun loungers, and a thatched lapa with a braai made for long Cape evenings. Secure parking behind the gate, fast Wi-Fi throughout, and the wine route on your doorstep.
