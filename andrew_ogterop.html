Hi, I’m Andrew.

I was born and raised in Cape Town and got my first camera at ten. I studied Fine Art at UCT’s Michaelis School, graduating in 2015 with photography as my major, and I’ve also done extra courses in darkroom, car and real estate photography.

I’d rather catch a moment than set one up. Smiles look better when they aren’t posed, so I keep things relaxed, chat to people and let the day happen. I’m fussy about the details in every frame.

I’ve photographed around 20 weddings, along with conferences, launches, villas, matric dances, 21sts and proposals, from Cape Point to Franschhoek.

Good to know

Can you do photo and video at the same event?

Yes. I shoot both myself, so you deal with one person from the first chat to the final files. I can film in 4K or 8K, steadied on a gimbal.

How soon will I see my photos?

I usually send the first photos within a day or two. The rest follow once they’re edited.

Do you travel outside Cape Town?

Yes. I’ve shot in Paarl, Stellenbosch and Franschhoek. The travel fees are listed above, and for anywhere further, just ask.

Can you cover an event that runs over several days?

Yes. I covered SYSPRO’s four-day conference in Franschhoek and Stellenbosch, and the three-day Negotiation Championship at the Graduate School of Business.
