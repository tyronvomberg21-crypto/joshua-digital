About

Hi, I'm Andrew.

I've been making pictures since I got my first camera at ten. I have a Fine Art degree from UCT's Michaelis School (2015), where photography became my major, plus extra training in darkroom, car and real estate photography.

I love the in-between moments: the laugh before the pose, the hug nobody planned. Smiles look better when they aren't posed. I'm specific about the details in every frame, I make people feel at ease, and I usually send the first work back within a day or two.

Since 2021: around 20 weddings, plus conferences, launches, luxury villas, matric dances, proposals and VIP tours, from the 12 Apostles to the V&A.
