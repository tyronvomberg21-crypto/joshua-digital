The Salon

Good hair, grounded

Tucked into Adelphi Centre on Sea Point's Main Road, Rooted is a contemporary salon with a warm, unhurried feel. We cut, colour and care for every kind of hair — and we start by actually listening to what you want.

From a clean fresh trim to a full balayage transformation, our stylists take the time to get it right, with honest advice and a finish you'll want to show off. No ego, no rush — just great hair and good energy.

Come put down roots with us — Shop 22, first floor, next to Dis-Chem.

— The Rooted team 🌿

Good To Know

Questions, answered

Where exactly are you?
Adelphi Centre (Adelphi Galleria), 127 Main Road, Sea Point — Shop 22 on the first floor, next to Dis-Chem.
Do you do all hair types?
Yes — straight, wavy, curly, coily, all of it. We tailor the cut, colour and care to your hair.
How do I book?
Easiest is a quick WhatsApp or a DM on Instagram. For colour, send a photo of your current hair and we'll advise.
Do you take walk-ins?
For quick cuts, often yes — but booking ahead means no waiting, especially on weekends and for colour.
