The plain answer

What is a custom system?

A custom system is the admin behind your business: everything that happens after a customer fills in a form or sends a message. In most small businesses that admin is the owner, retyping the same details from WhatsApp into a spreadsheet, then into a quote, then into an invoice, and keeping track of where each job is by memory. The system takes that over. One intake or booking form lands where it should, every customer gets a record you own with the full history of their jobs, the confirmations, reminders, follow-ups and invoices go out on their own, and a simple dashboard shows you the numbers you check every week.

It's built around how you actually work, not the other way round, and it works with your website or on its own. If you don't have a website yet, I build those too, from R6,500: see web design in Cape Town, or the work.

Plumbing

What can it connect to?

A system works with your website or on its own. Forms land where you need them: WhatsApp, email, your customer list. If the system sits behind a website I build, that site can take bookings through the tools you already use, such as Fresha, Setmore or NightsBridge, run a shop on Shopify, and take card payments through Yoco or PayFast, with money straight to your bank; and if you use none of those, enquiries come straight to your WhatsApp. The website side is what I do all day: 35+ concept sites, plus the shop built for Café Chiffon in Green Point.

One rule I keep: I never promise an integration I haven't tested. If your tool isn't on this list, I check it before it goes into the scope, not after. Under the hood it's hand-coded web pages and forms, with ready-made automation tools where that's faster than building it from scratch.

Common questions

What's the difference between a custom system and a Shop & Booking site?
A Shop & Booking site (from R12,000) is the front: your customers order, book and pay online, with money straight to your bank. A custom system (from R30,000) is the admin behind it: what happens after the order or enquiry lands, the job's stages, the customer's record, the reminders and invoices, the weekly numbers. Plenty of businesses only need the site, and if that's you I'll say so on the call. See the booking website and online shop pages.
Do I pay anything for the call or the scope?
No. The 30-minute call and the written scope cost nothing, and nothing is owed until you say yes to the scope. Then it's 50% to start and 50% at handover, with the price fixed in writing before anything begins.
How long does a custom system take to build?
Longer than a website, which I aim to have live within about seven days of having your content. A system is built in stages, and the written scope lists those stages and a start date, so you know before you say yes. If my next weeks are full I say so and give you an honest date rather than a hopeful one. You see each stage working on real-looking dummy data before the next one begins.
What does the care plan cost for a system?
It's sized to the system, R950 to R1,500 a month, and runs from month one. It covers managing the hosting and any automation subscriptions, a monthly check that everything works, and up to about two hours of changes a month. The website care plan, at R450 a month, is explained on the pricing page.
What happens to the system if you're not around one day?
It keeps running. You have a copy of the code from handover, and every account it runs on is in your name, so nothing switches off and any developer can carry on from your copy. The same answer applies to the sites I build: domain in your name, a copy of the files, and your own editor.
