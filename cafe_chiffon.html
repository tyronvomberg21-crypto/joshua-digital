Handmade, by hand, every day

Small-batch cakes with an Asian-inspired soul.

Café Chiffon is a little dessert studio in Green Point where light Japanese-style sponge meets silky buttercream and the kind of finishing that takes real patience — fresh fruit, edible flowers, a careful swirl on top.

The menu changes with the season, so there's always something new in the display fridge. Pair a slice with a matcha latte or a slow specialty coffee, and you've found the prettiest table in the Cape Quarter.

No bookings, no fuss — just walk in. And when you want something for a birthday or a celebration, we make custom cakes to order.
