The House

A home with a view, run by the people who live it

Kalk Bay Guest House sits up the hill off Boyes Drive, looking down over its own pool and garden to the working harbour and the whole sweep of False Bay. Costa and Ashleigh host it themselves — the kind of place where the hosts organise your transfers, point you at the right restaurant, and know which mornings the sunrise is worth setting an alarm for.

Two private sea-view suites, a pool to come home to, birdsong in the garden, and everything Kalk Bay is famous for a few minutes' walk away. Thirty-five minutes from the city, a world away in feel.

The Village

Everything Kalk Bay is famous for, downhill from your door

A working fishing harbour where the boats still come in with the catch. A fish market on the quay. Antique shops, galleries and coffee spots along Main Road. Tidal pools, the Brass Bell on the rocks, and Boulders' penguins a short drive down the coast.

You're not near Kalk Bay. You're in it — above it, with the best seat in the village.

The HarbourFresh fish off the boats, Kalky's fish & chips on the quay, and the morning bustle of a real working harbour.
Eat & BrowseHarbour House and the Brass Bell for seafood, antique shops and galleries along Main Road, coffee with a view.
Sea & CoastTidal pools, swims off the rocks, whale-watching in season, and the Boulders penguins just down the coast.
