Meet your planner

Hello, I'm Lucille

I plan weddings, elopements and celebrations across Cape Town and the Winelands — from the very first mood-board to the final send-off under the stars.

My couples come to me for calm. For someone who has walked every timeline a hundred times, who knows the venues and the suppliers, and who quietly makes sure that on your wedding day there is nothing left to worry about — only to enjoy.

Whether it's an intimate elopement or a full Winelands celebration, I take on a limited number of weddings each season so that yours is given all of me.
