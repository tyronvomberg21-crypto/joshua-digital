The Place

The guesthouse in Cape Town's south

Pro Moni's sits in Marina da Gama, the waterside village on the Zandvlei estuary — a quiet pocket of the peninsula where flamingos feed on the vlei and the mountains stand behind the palm trees.

The three suites are bright, modern and fully self-catering: your own kitchenette, en-suite shower, air-conditioning, satellite TV and free Wi-Fi. Solar power keeps everything running through any load-shedding.

Surfers, birders, families visiting the south peninsula — guests keep coming back, and 314 reviews say why.
