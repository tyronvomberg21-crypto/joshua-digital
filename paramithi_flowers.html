Our story

Flowers will be
my autobiography

Paramithi began in 2010 with a single belief — that flowers are never just decoration. They hold feeling. They remember. They tell the story of two people on the most tender day of their lives.

Every arch, aisle and table is designed by hand, one stem at a time, in the palette of your own romance. Soft, wild, abundant — never stiff, never off-the-shelf. We work with a handful of couples each season so your day is given all of us.

Let's write yours in blooms.

Jeanette
