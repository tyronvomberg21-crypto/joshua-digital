Welcome

Warm brick, wooden ceilings, and a proper welcome

Quest is a family-run bed & breakfast in Melkbosstrand — the laid-back beach village on Cape Town's West Coast where the sunsets are famous and Table Mountain sits on the horizon.

Inside it's all warmth: face-brick walls, timber ceilings, comfortable beds and a courtyard pool with its own fountain. For a decade, guests have arrived as bookings and left as regulars.
