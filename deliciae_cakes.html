My story

Hi, I'm Nubira.

I'm a self-taught home baker in Milnerton, Cape Town, with a serious love for buttercream florals. What started as a hobby became Deliciae Cakes — little edible works of art for life's sweetest moments.

Every cake and cupcake is piped by hand, made fresh to order, and designed just for you. No two are ever quite the same. 🌷
