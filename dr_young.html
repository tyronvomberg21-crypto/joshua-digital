Straight answers

Okay, but what actually is lip filler?

Dr Young breaks it down. No complicated terminology, no unnecessary hype. Just what the treatment involves, so you can decide.

What is lip filler made of?

Most lip fillers are hyaluronic acid, a substance your body already makes. It holds moisture and adds a little shape and volume where it's placed.

Will it look natural?

That's the whole idea. You talk through how much you want first, and Dr Young will tell you honestly what suits your face. Less is often more.

Is Botox only for wrinkles?

No. It's also used for jaw clenching and TMJ, and to reduce heavy underarm sweating. Your consultation is where you find out if it's right for you.

Why see a dentist for facial aesthetics?

Dentists work with the muscles, nerves and structure of the face every day. That knowledge of facial anatomy is the foundation for safe, subtle results.

Can the treatment come to me?

Yes. Ask about home visits: comfort, convenience and the same professional care, without leaving your home.
