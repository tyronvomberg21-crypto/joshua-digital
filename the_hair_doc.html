Questions, answered

Where are you based?
In-chair appointments are at 93 Hengelaar Street, Cape Town. For the Premium Mobile Grooming, the Doc comes to you.
What are your hours?
Wednesday to Saturday, 7am–6pm. Closed Sunday to Tuesday. Book ahead to lock in your slot.
How do I book?
Easiest is to book online in seconds, or send a WhatsApp. House calls are by appointment — just message to arrange a time.
Do you do beard work?
Definitely — the Fade with Beard (R160 + R20) includes a full beard shape and line-up for the complete look.
