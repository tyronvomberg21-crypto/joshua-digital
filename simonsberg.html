Welcome

The Winelands, hosted the old way

Simonsberg Guest House sits in a quiet cul-de-sac on the foothills of Simonsberg — five minutes from the oaks and cafés of historic Stellenbosch, and surrounded by the wine farms that made this valley famous.

It's been run with heart since 2008. Breakfast is served in the leafy garden, the pool sparkles in summer, and your host Elize is the reason so many guest reviews read like thank-you letters.
