Our story

A home bakery, baking with heart

The Cake Lot is a home bakery in Ottery, Cape Town, turning out handmade cakes and treats that taste as good as they look. Every order is baked fresh, to order, and finished by hand.

No two cakes are the same — just tell us what you're celebrating, and we'll bake something worth gathering around. That's the whole idea: love at first bite.
