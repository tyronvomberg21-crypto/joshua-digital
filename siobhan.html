How I work

The same four steps on every job, whatever the size of the business.

  1. I look

    At your business first, not your pages. What you want, what you actually need, and where those two don't quite match. That's where every job starts.

  2. I build the structure

    A structure for your marketing, made for your business and nobody else's. What goes where, what it says, how the pieces hold together. Not a package off a shelf.

  3. I propose it

    You see the whole thing before anything goes out, with a quote for that job. Ask anything, change anything. Then you decide.

  4. I do the work

    Then I do it myself. The writing, the content, the photos. Nothing gets handed to someone you haven't met. The person you spoke to is the person doing the work.
