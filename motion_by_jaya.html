Behind the Lens

Hi, I'm Jaya

I'm a Cape Town based videographer and content creator with one obsession: making things look and feel incredible on screen. Whether it's a brand that needs to stand out, a wedding that deserves to be remembered, or social content that actually stops the scroll — I shoot it all.

Every project gets the same treatment: a proper understanding of your vision, a sharp eye on set, and an edit that brings it to life. No cookie-cutter clips — just motion that means something.

If it moves, I'll make it move beautifully. Let's create something.
