Welcome

Make yourself at home — Bozena insists

35 on Washington is a family-run guest house in the quiet, leafy suburb of Boston, Bellville. Behind the gate you'll find a garden filled with roses and lavender, a small sparkling pool, and six rooms decorated in a warm blend of antique and modern.

It's the kind of place where breakfast is cooked, not catered — and where your host knows your name by the second morning.
