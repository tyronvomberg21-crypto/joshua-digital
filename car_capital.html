How it works

Four steps. Most clients are paid the same day they drive in.

  1. WhatsApp your car

    A photo, the registration and the mileage. We reply with a cash range, usually within the hour.

  2. Pick a term

    30, 60 or 90 days to buy it back. The term is set up front and can't be changed later.

  3. Drive in

    Bring the car and your documents to Strand Street. We inspect and make a final offer.

  4. Get paid

    Sign and the money is sent. Buy the car back any time in your term, no early penalty.

Questions

How do I book a valuation?

WhatsApp us a photo and your registration, or fill in the form. We'll give you a range, then book a time at our Assessment Centre at 120 Strand Street for the final quote.

What does it cost?

On a car valued at R1 000 000:

60 daysHolding 10% · insurance and storage 4% · admin and re-reg as aboveR852 002 paid to you
90 daysHolding 15% · insurance and storage 6% · admin and re-reg as aboveR782 002 paid to you

You buy the car back at the valued amount. No early-repurchase penalty.

What if I don't buy it back in time?

If the vehicle isn't repurchased within the term you chose up front, it is sold to a third party. We send an email reminder before the date.

Do you buy new and used?

Yes, as long as the vehicle meets our minimum valuation criteria. Bring the car, the registration papers and your ID.

Where are you and when are you open?
