How it works

Four steps.

  1. WhatsApp your car

    A photo, the registration and the mileage. We reply with a cash range.

  2. Pick a term

    30, 60 or 90 days to buy it back. The term is set up front and can't be changed later.

  3. Drive in

    Bring the car and your documents to Strand Street. We inspect and make a final offer. Your car stays with us, insured and stored, until you buy it back.

  4. Get paid

    Sign and the money is sent. Buy the car back any time in your term, no early penalty.

What if I don't buy it back in time?

If the vehicle isn't repurchased within the term you chose up front, it is sold to a third party. You get an email reminder before the date.

Do you buy new and used?

Yes, as long as the vehicle meets the minimum valuation criteria. Bring the car, the registration papers and your ID.

How do I get a valuation?

WhatsApp a photo and your registration, or call 021 205 8152. You get a range, then book a time at 120 Strand Street for the final quote.
