The House

A little jewel on the West Coast

Mt Bijoux sits right on the Bloubergstrand beachfront, looking across the bay to one of the most photographed views on earth — Table Mountain, rising straight out of the Atlantic.

Inside, eight individually decorated rooms bring a quiet, classic opulence: pure cotton linen, air-conditioning, and the kind of detail that makes a stay feel like a gift to yourself. Solar power keeps everything running, whatever the grid is doing.

It is the sort of place guests book for one night and extend to three.
