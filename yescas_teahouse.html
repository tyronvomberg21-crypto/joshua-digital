Owner-run, made from scratch

Not just tea. A proper Cantonese teahouse.

Teahouses have always been where Cantonese families meet: a pot of tea, a table of small plates, and nobody in a hurry. Liz runs Yesca's the same way. Every wrapper is rolled here, every filling is seasoned here, and she'll happily walk you through the menu if it's your first time.

Handmade daily

Dumplings, bao and spring rolls made in-house from scratch, steamed or fried to order.

Colour-coded

Each filling gets its own naturally coloured wrapper, so you can tell them apart at a glance.

Good ingredients

Free-range meat, organic veg from Love in a Bowl, no MSG and no preservatives.

Everyone's welcome

Plenty of vegan dishes, gluten-free wrappers, and dogs are welcome on the patio.

Workshops with Liz

Kintsugi: mending with gold.

Kintsugi is the Japanese art of repairing broken pottery with gold, so the cracks become the most beautiful part. Liz runs small morning workshops at the teahouse that are as much about slowing down as about the craft.

  1. A morning of mindfulness and breathwork
  2. Learn to mend a piece with gold, step by step
  3. A taster lunch of dim sum, chow mein and mooncakes
